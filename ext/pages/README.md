# pages

Server-rendered multi-page apps from a `pb_pages` folder, PocketPages-compatible, with routing and templates in Go.

**Runs on:** Single app, or many under [pocker](../pocker/README.md).

> feature. See [Create your own plugin](../../docs/plugins.md).
